I am a B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML) at United Institute of Technology in India. I build intelligent, practical, and well-designed digital products, and I treat software, hardware, and design as parts of the same craft. A good idea needs solid engineering behind it, and a solid engineering project needs an interface people enjoy using.

On the technical side, I work across C, C++, Java, Python, and JavaScript, and I build responsive interfaces with HTML5 and CSS3. I store and query data with SQL and MongoDB, and I have used or explored Linux, Ubuntu, Kali Linux, macOS, Windows, Android, and iOS, which keeps me comfortable across different environments and command-line workflows.

My interests reach into robotics, where software meets sensors and motors. I am drawn to Arduino and ESP32 projects, including obstacle-avoidance robot cars, and to computer vision and gesture-controlled systems that connect AI with the physical world. Several of my projects already use webcam hand tracking to turn movement into interaction.

Creatively, I work in video editing, thumbnail and graphic design, photo editing, and scriptwriting. That background shapes how I build: I think about how a project looks, how it flows, and how it tells its story, not only whether the code runs.

Right now I am focused on AI-powered applications, interactive web projects, and gesture-driven tools. I am also exploring AI-assisted development with Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, and Google Antigravity, and I am building a portfolio of projects that show both the code and the design behind them. My long-term goal is to grow into a versatile technology professional who turns ideas into useful, user-focused solutions, and to contribute to innovative projects alongside developer communities.
